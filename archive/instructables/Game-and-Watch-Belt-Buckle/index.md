## Step 2: Gluing Stuff Together

![Step 2: Gluing Stuff Together image 1](images/step03_01.jpg)

![Step 2: Gluing Stuff Together image 2](images/step03_02.jpg)

![Step 2: Gluing Stuff Together image 3](images/step03_03.jpg)

![Step 2: Gluing Stuff Together image 4](images/step03_04.jpg)

You have probably guessed by now that this isn't a hard ible'.   Matter of fact is damn simple!  Steps: 1. Use something sharp like a screwdriver and scratch the surface of the belt.  This will help the glue stick better.  2.  Add some strong glue and stick on the G&W.  Try and position it so it is level with the top of the buckle and straight.  3.  Leave to dry for 24 hours.
