## Step 2: Practice Making Fire

![Step 2: Practice Making Fire image 1](images/step03_01.jpg)

![Step 2: Practice Making Fire image 2](images/step03_02.jpg)

![Step 2: Practice Making Fire image 3](images/step03_03.jpg)

![Step 2: Practice Making Fire image 4](images/step03_04.jpg)

![Step 2: Practice Making Fire image 5](images/step03_05.jpg)

First thing I would do is to practice igniting the steel wool.  To do this you need to do the following:1.  Grab the steel wool and pull the threads so it opens up a little.2.  Next place one end of the battery on the wool and touch the steel wool to the other end of the battery.3.  You should instantly see sparks and the steel wool start to light.  If not, touch the battery terminals again.  4.  Once the wool starts to ignite it should just continue to smolder and burn.  If not, just blow on it a little and it will blaze up.


## Step 3: Prepare the Steel Wool

![Step 3: Prepare the Steel Wool image 1](images/step04_01.jpg)

Steps:1.  Garb the steel wool and carefully unwind a piece.2.  If it seems a little thick, you can just pull it apart.3.  You should end up with a small rectangle piece of steel wool.


## Step 4: Prepare the Battery

![Step 4: Prepare the Battery image 1](images/step05_01.jpg)

![Step 4: Prepare the Battery image 2](images/step05_02.jpg)

![Step 4: Prepare the Battery image 3](images/step05_03.jpg)

![Step 4: Prepare the Battery image 4](images/step05_04.jpg)

Steps:1.  So the steel wool doesn't ignite the wool inside the container, you need to wrap it in some electrical tape.2.  Take a piece of tape and wrap it around the ends of the terminals a couple of times.
