Junkyard Fish-bone Sculpture

View on Instructables →

INTROIntroduction

STEP 1Step 1: Step 1 - Materials and Tools

So the first thing you need to source is some rusty, old prices of metal. The best places to find this stuff is at tips, old or abandoned houses, the sides of the road, actually anywhere!

If your married like I am then you might have to work on a reason why you need to stop the car every time you see an old piece of thrown away, rusted and useless (not for much longer though) junk.

Materials:

  • Rusted sheet metal - I used a 20 lt can I found.
  • Rusted wire. This is attached to the fish and used to display it.
  • Thin rusted wire. This is used to attach the fish to the display wire.

Tools:

  • Tin snips.
  • Sharp, pointy things (used to scratch your design into the sheet metal.
  • Various pliers

STEP 2Step 2: Step - 2 Design and Cutting

Now you have everything your ready to scratch in your design.

Steps.

  • Cut-out a nice square piece of metal from whatever you have managed to find.
  • Use something sharp to scratch in the areas you will need to cut. When you come to the head, it's not too important to have everything symmetrical. You can fix this up once the general design has been cut-out.
  • The sketch below is what I worked from.
  • Use a ruler to make sure your ribs are nice and straight.
  • Start cutting.

STEP 3Step 3: Step - 3 Finessing

Once you have the general design cut out you need to trim all of the excess bits and make the head piece symmetrical.

  • Use a pair of pliers to remove all of the excess rib cut-outs that you don't need. I found that If I grabbed the piece of metal that I   wanted to remove and bent it back and forth it would remove it cleanly and evenly.
  • Push the 2 parts of the head together and cut them so they are the same. Also cut a couple of tiny triangles for the mouth on both parts of the head.
  • Drill a couple of holes for the eyes.

STEP 4Step 4: Step 4 - Bending Into Shape and Attaching

Next you need to get everything bent into shape.

  • This part is up to you on how you want your sculpture to look. Make the ribs longer or shorter depending on how fat you want your fish to look.
  • Slightly make each part of the head curved and bring the front parts together.
  • Curve the spine to give the fish an arched back.
  • Twist the tail so it is vertical.

Next is to add the thick wire which the fish will sit on.

  • Drill 2 holes along the middle of the spine.
  • Bend the metal wire into a "U" shape and thread through the 2 holes.
  • Once through, bend the wire so it won't come out.

Your done! Go and stick him in the garden somewhere where everyone can see him.

STEP 5Step 5: Step 4 - Make More!

So now you have done your first, experiment and try some different styles and sizes. Below are a couple of others that I made the same day.

One was slightly smaller than the first one and is mounted on a rusted, round wheel from a pulley. I welded the wire to the wheel and wired the fish onto the thick display wire.

The other was a tiny one, no bigger than a 50 cent coin. It was quite fiddly but looks really cool. I glued the fish to the wire stand and mounted it into a piece of wood.
