Now that you have everything together, it's just a matter of  using some cable ties to secure the cord along the handle of the lantern.

I used the electric cable itself to hang the lamp, once the cables are on there's no danger of it pulling it out of the light socket.

Considerations

  • You could easily rig this up with a solar light such as the ones you use in your garden.
  • What about adding one of those candle globes that flicker?
  • You could add a sensor so it turns on as soon as someone approaches.
