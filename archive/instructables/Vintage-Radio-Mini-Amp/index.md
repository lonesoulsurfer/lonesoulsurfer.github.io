The first step is to pull apart your vintage radio.   Steps.  1. Take off the cowling.  Mine was held together by a single screw on the back.  2. Remove the insides.  Before you start to take things apart, take a good look at everything and think about what you want to keep and will need.  My radio was well engineered and it was a bit tricky trying to get the electronic out but I managed it in the end.  3. Store the parts somewhere safe, you’ll find that you need some later on.  4. Remove any dials, badges etc so you can give everything a good clean.

![Step 3: Cleaning the Radio image 1](images/step04_01.jpg)

Now it's time to give your radio a good clean!  The one I purchased had a lot of dirt on it that had built up over the years,  Steps:  1.  Use hot soapy water to wipe down the cowl and front cover.  2.  Use ear cleaners to get into the hard to reach places.  3.  Wipe down with a towel to dry any excess water.


## Step 4: Taking Apart the Mini Amp

![Step 4: Taking Apart the Mini Amp image 1](images/step05_01.jpg)

![Step 4: Taking Apart the Mini Amp image 2](images/step05_02.jpg)

![Step 4: Taking Apart the Mini Amp image 3](images/step05_03.jpg)

![Step 4: Taking Apart the Mini Amp image 4](images/step05_04.jpg)

![Step 4: Taking Apart the Mini Amp image 5](images/step05_05.jpg)

![Step 4: Taking Apart the Mini Amp image 6](images/step05_06.jpg)

Steps:1.  First take off the knobs from the front of the amp2.  Next un-screw the back 3.  Once you have the back off there are a bunch of small screws inside- un-do all of these, including the ones holding the speaker in place4.  Un-do the bolts holding the Potentiometers into place on the front cowl.5.  Carefully pull all of the bits out


## Step 5: Adding the Speaker Cover and Speaker

![Step 5: Adding the Speaker Cover and Speaker image 1](images/step06_01.jpg)

![Step 5: Adding the Speaker Cover and Speaker image 2](images/step06_02.jpg)

![Step 5: Adding the Speaker Cover and Speaker image 3](images/step06_03.jpg)

![Step 5: Adding the Speaker Cover and Speaker image 4](images/step06_04.jpg)

![Step 5: Adding the Speaker Cover and Speaker image 5](images/step06_05.jpg)

Steps:1.  Remove the speaker cover from the amp.  You need to glue this down on the back of the radio where the original speaker was.  I used used super glue.2.  Un-solder the wires from the speaker and add some longer ones.  This will make things a lot easier when replacing the battery.3.  Reattach the speaker to the speaker cover.


## Step 6: Drill the Holes for the Knobs Etc

![Step 6: Drill the Holes for the Knobs Etc image 1](images/step07_01.jpg)

![Step 6: Drill the Holes for the Knobs Etc image 2](images/step07_02.jpg)

![Step 6: Drill the Holes for the Knobs Etc image 3](images/step07_03.jpg)

![Step 6: Drill the Holes for the Knobs Etc image 4](images/step07_04.jpg)

![Step 6: Drill the Holes for the Knobs Etc image 5](images/step07_05.jpg)

![Step 6: Drill the Holes for the Knobs Etc image 6](images/step07_06.jpg)

![Step 6: Drill the Holes for the Knobs Etc image 7](images/step07_07.jpg)

![Step 6: Drill the Holes for the Knobs Etc image 8](images/step07_08.jpg)

Steps:1.  Take off the decal from the amp where the knobs were and use this as a template.2.  Decide where you want the knobs to go making sure that they don't get in the way when you are carrying the amp/radio.3.  Drill out the holes and once done attach the volume control etc to the radio.  Just use the nuts that came off the Potentiometers to reattach.4.  Mark out and drill holes for the AV and headphone jack.  This is a little tricky as the AV jack is square.  Take your time and use a dremmel to remove.  5.  Screw into place.


## Step 7: Add the Battery Holder.

![Step 7: Add the Battery Holder. image 1](images/step08_01.jpg)

![Step 7: Add the Battery Holder. image 2](images/step08_02.jpg)

![Step 7: Add the Battery Holder. image 3](images/step08_03.jpg)

![Step 7: Add the Battery Holder. image 4](images/step08_04.jpg)

![Step 7: Add the Battery Holder. image 5](images/step08_05.jpg)

Steps:1.  Cut the wires from the battery holder on the amp.2.  Solder on the battery holder making sure the polarities are right3.  Add some velcro to the bottom of the holder and attach to the inside of the radio.


## Step 8: Add the Knobs

![Step 8: Add the Knobs image 1](images/step09_01.jpg)

![Step 8: Add the Knobs image 2](images/step09_02.jpg)

Steps:1.  Its up to you whether you want to use the same knobs or not.  The knobs suited my radio so I went with the ones that came with the amp.2.  Simply push back on the knobs to the end of the Potentiometers.
