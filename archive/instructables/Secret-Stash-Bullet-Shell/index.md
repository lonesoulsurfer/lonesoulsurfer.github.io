Yes I know that the title is a little provocative but that's what this is called (I think...)Steps:1.  Remove the washer and fastener from the switch2.  What you will be doing is soldering the washer to the fastener.  Add a small amount of flux to the fastener and place on top of the washer3.  Heat up the washer/fastener with the mini blowtorch and add some solder4.  Done


## Step 3: Soldering the Flange to the Bullet Case

![Step 3: Soldering the Flange to the Bullet Case image 1](images/step04_01.jpg)

![Step 3: Soldering the Flange to the Bullet Case image 2](images/step04_02.jpg)

![Step 3: Soldering the Flange to the Bullet Case image 3](images/step04_03.jpg)

![Step 3: Soldering the Flange to the Bullet Case image 4](images/step04_04.jpg)

Steps.1.  Place the flange so the washer sits on top of the bullet as shown below.  Make sure you add a little flux to the top of the bullet2.  Next place on a hard metal surface with the washer on the bottom.  Heat up and add some solder.3.  Done.  Now you have added the female screw section to the bullet.


## Step 4: Bullet Section - Part 1

![Step 4: Bullet Section - Part 1 image 1](images/step05_01.jpg)

Next you will need to modify the switch for it too be used as the male section for the bullet head.Steps:1.  With a pair of wire cutters, cut the top section of the switch.  This will help make sure that the drill bit sits correctly when you start to drill the section out.2.  Place the switch in  a vice and drill out the centre section.  This comes out pretty easily.


## Step 5: Bullet Section - Part 2

![Step 5: Bullet Section - Part 2 image 1](images/step06_01.jpg)

![Step 5: Bullet Section - Part 2 image 2](images/step06_02.jpg)

![Step 5: Bullet Section - Part 2 image 3](images/step06_03.jpg)

![Step 5: Bullet Section - Part 2 image 4](images/step06_04.jpg)

![Step 5: Bullet Section - Part 2 image 5](images/step06_05.jpg)

![Step 5: Bullet Section - Part 2 image 6](images/step06_06.jpg)

In order for the dome nut to sit flush on the bullet, you need too remove the flange on the screw section.Steps:1.  Remove the flange with a small cutting wheel or hack saw2.  File the section so it is flat and smooth3.  Add some flux to the filed section and add it to the dome nut.  4.  Clamp it together, heat-up and add some solder.5.  Done

So you should now be able to screw the dome nut into the bullet case.  If you wanted to stop here you could just leave it as is.  I decided to give it a polish and add a loop so I could add it to a key ring or necklace.
