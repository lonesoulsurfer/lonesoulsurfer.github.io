Secret Stash Bullet Shell

View on Instructables →

INTROIntroduction

This project only was made with only 3 parts.

The end result in my humble opinion, turned out pretty damn good!

I have been thinking about how to turn a bullet into a secret container and had a few ideas which I tried but didn't get the look I was after.

After some frustration I went back to basics and looked around in my electronic bins to see what I could use.

I found a push on/off switch and had one of those eureka moments.

The main tool you will need in this project is a mini blow torch as there is some soldering that will need to be done. Other then that, all other tools can be improvised.

So what do you keep in a bullet secret stash!

Anything that fits...

Actually there isn't a lot of room inside a 444 magnum shell so whatever you decide to add,, it will have to be small.

I keep a rolled-up $5 note in mine, but you could easily keep a small memento, matches, small survival kit (not a bad idea actually) or whatever takes your fancy.

So without further ado - Here is my Secret Stash Bullet Shell

STEP 1Step 1: Things to Gather

Parts:

  1. 44 magnum bullet casing - Etsy
  1. Switch - eBay
  1. Small piece of copper wire (for the loop)
  1. Dome nut (size M6 or 1/4 inch) - eBay

Tools:

  1. Small blow torch
  1. Soldering iron
  1. Flux
  1. Various pliers
  1. Drill
  1. Safety glasses
  1. Small files

STEP 2Step 2: Female Flange Section

Yes I know that the title is a little provocative but that's what this is called (I think...)

Steps:

  1. Remove the washer and fastener from the switch
  1. What you will be doing is soldering the washer to the fastener. Add a small amount of flux to the fastener and place on top of the washer
  1. Heat up the washer/fastener with the mini blowtorch and add some solder
  1. Done

STEP 3Step 3: Soldering the Flange to the Bullet Case

Steps.

  1. Place the flange so the washer sits on top of the bullet as shown below. Make sure you add a little flux to the top of the bullet
  1. Next place on a hard metal surface with the washer on the bottom. Heat up and add some solder.
  1. Done. Now you have added the female screw section to the bullet.

STEP 4Step 4: Bullet Section - Part 1

Next you will need to modify the switch for it too be used as the male section for the bullet head.

Steps:

  1. With a pair of wire cutters, cut the top section of the switch. This will help make sure that the drill bit sits correctly when you start to drill the section out.
  1. Place the switch in a vice and drill out the centre section. This comes out pretty easily.

STEP 5Step 5: Bullet Section - Part 2

In order for the dome nut to sit flush on the bullet, you need too remove the flange on the screw section.

Steps:

  1. Remove the flange with a small cutting wheel or hack saw
  1. File the section so it is flat and smooth
  1. Add some flux to the filed section and add it to the dome nut.
  1. Clamp it together, heat-up and add some solder.
  1. Done

STEP 6Step 6: Test

So you should now be able to screw the dome nut into the bullet case.

If you wanted to stop here you could just leave it as is.

I decided to give it a polish and add a loop so I could add it to a key ring or necklace.

STEP 7Step 7: Adding a Loop

Steps:

  1. Twist a piece of copper wire to make a loop and arm section.
  1. Next add some flux and solder to the bottom of the bullet.

STEP 8Step 8: Give It a Polish

Steps:

  1. Use some metal polish and give the bullet a good going over.
  1. I used a general metal polish and it worked a treat.
