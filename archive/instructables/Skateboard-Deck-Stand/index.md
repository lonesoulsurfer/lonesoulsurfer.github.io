![Step 2: Stripping the Deck image 6](images/step03_06.jpg)

Steps:1.       The first thing you’ll need to do if you have an skateboard is to remove the trucks.  Unscrew the 4 bolts on each truck and remove the truck.2.       Next you will need to remove the grip tape.  This can be a bitch to remove so be patient and carefully peel away.  I have done this before and there has been no glue residue left on the board.  Other times I’m left with a sticky mess – this was one of those times.3.       To remove the glue, just add a little fuel (petrol will do fine) to the deck, leave for a bit and then rub the glue off.  You will need to do this a few times to remove all of the glue


## Step 3: Sanding the Deck

![Step 3: Sanding the Deck image 1](images/step04_01.jpg)

![Step 3: Sanding the Deck image 2](images/step04_02.jpg)

If you are using a used deck, most likely it has some damage on the ends from all those kick flips, ollies and kick turns.  Not to worry though as this will add some character to your board.  Sanding will also help to remove some of the larger scuffs and also water marks.Steps:1.       Grab your sander and add some 80 grit sandpaper and start to sand.  This will help remove any divots, marks etc.  don’t forget to sand the edges as well2.       Move onto a lighter grit paper and repeat until the board is smooth and clean.3.       Wipe away the sawdust and get it ready for waxing

I decided to just add some bees wax to the board to bring out the grain.  You could stain or just add a clear coat if you wanted to.Steps:1.       Add some wax to a cloth and rub into the wood. 2.       Do one side first and make sure that you remove any excess wax with a clean cloth3.       Do the other side and repeat if necessary

Steps:1.       Screw the Tee into the 450mm pipe.2.       Screw the 100mm pipe into the ends of the Tee3.       Screw the caps onto the end of the 100mm pipe4.       Screw the flange into the top of the 450mm pipe5.       Lastly, make sure you tighten up everything well.  Use a vice or grip pliers to ensure everything is nice and tightly joined.


## Step 6: Painting the Legs

![Step 6: Painting the Legs image 1](images/step07_01.jpg)

![Step 6: Painting the Legs image 2](images/step07_02.jpg)

![Step 6: Painting the Legs image 3](images/step07_03.jpg)

![Step 6: Painting the Legs image 4](images/step07_04.jpg)

Steps:1.       Place the legs so they are standing up on the flanges.  Make sure you do this outside.2.       Next spray them with the matte black spray-paint and leave to dry.  It should dry very fast.3.       Give the legs a good amount of coats – I did about 5 on each.4.       Leave to dry for 4-5 hours

The good news about attaching the legs is 2 of the holes in the deck from the trucks will line-up with the flange.  This means that you only have to drill a couple of holes for the other 2 holes.  You can use putty to fill the other 2 holes in the deck but I decided to leave them.Steps:1.       Drill out the tops of the 2 holes that you are going to use so the top of the screw fits flush with the deck.2.       Add a couple of screws and bolts and secure3.       Next, flip the deck over and drill a couple of holes into the deck using the flange holes as guides.4.       Drill out the holes and add the screws – the same as the other 25.       Do this for the other legDone!
